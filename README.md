# NestWorth

> A simple, visual wealth tracker for Android — built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.

NestWorth helps you build a clearer picture of your personal finances by tracking income, expenses, savings rate, assets, liabilities, and overall net worth in one place.

The home screen gives you a quick financial snapshot, including:

- Current net worth
- Monthly net-worth change
- 30-day savings rate
- Top assets
- Recent financial milestones/trophies
- Quick actions for logging income and expenses
- Navigation to your assets and transaction history

## ✨ Features

### 💰 Income & Expense Tracking
Log your income and expenses to keep your financial activity organized and understand where your money is going.

### 📈 Net Worth Tracking
Track your overall net worth by combining the value of your assets and liabilities.

### 💵 Savings Rate
Monitor your savings rate over time and use it as a simple indicator of your financial progress.

### 🏦 Assets & Liabilities
Keep track of accounts, investments, property, debts, and other financial items that contribute to your net worth.

### 🏆 Financial Milestones
NestWorth includes a trophy system to make progress more engaging and encourage consistent financial habits.

### 📊 Financial Overview
The home dashboard surfaces the numbers that matter most so you can understand your financial position at a glance.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Kotlin**
- **Jetpack Compose**
- **Android**
- **Material Design / Compose UI**

## 🧭 App Structure

The app is centered around three main areas:

| Section | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | View your current financial snapshot and quick actions |
| **Assets** | Track assets and liabilities that make up your net worth |
| **History** | Review previously recorded income and expenses |

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites

- Android Studio
- Android SDK
- A compatible Android device or emulator
- JDK version required by the project's Gradle/Android configuration

### Installation

1. Clone the repository:

   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/YOUR_USERNAME/NestWorth.git
   cd NestWorth
   ```

2. Open the project in Android Studio.

3. Let Gradle sync and download the required dependencies.

4. Run the app on an Android emulator or connected device.

> Replace `YOUR_USERNAME/NestWorth` with the actual GitHub repository URL.

## 🎯 Project Goals

NestWorth is designed around a few simple principles:

- **Keep financial tracking simple** — logging money should be quick.
- **Make progress visible** — important financial metrics should be easy to understand.
- **Focus on net worth** — income and expenses are useful, but long-term wealth is the bigger picture.
- **Make consistency rewarding** — milestones and trophies help turn financial tracking into a habit.
